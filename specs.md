# Jump Boss Game

## Description

This game specs were created by my son Giulio, 6 years old. It's just some drawings on a pdf. Since he watched quite a bit of Among Us on Youtube, I imagine that is the influence so it should be a platform fighting game in that 2D graphic styles. Graphics are simple.
